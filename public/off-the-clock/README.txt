Put paintings and photographs here (jpg, png or webp), then list each one in
OFF_THE_CLOCK in src/lib/data.ts, for example:

  { src: "/off-the-clock/sunset.jpg", title: "Sunset over Lahore", kind: "photo", note: "Shot on my phone, 2025" },

Keep files under about 1 MB each (2000px on the long side is plenty).
